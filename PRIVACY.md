# Privacy policy — Saved Tab Groups

Saved Tab Groups is developed by daniele-f. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or developer-operated servers.

The extension reads tab titles, URLs, favicons, and native group information to display groups in the current window. When you choose Save & close and confirm, it stores the group's title, color, collapsed state, ordered tab titles and URLs, and snapshot identifier and timestamp in Firefox's local extension storage. Saved snapshots are removed after a successful restore. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage.

Saved snapshots are not sent to the developer or synchronized by this extension. Search runs locally. Website favicons supplied by Firefox may be loaded from their original URLs; those requests can contact the corresponding website. Restoring a group opens its websites normally, subject to those websites' privacy policies.

Permissions: storage saves inactive groups locally; tabs reads tab titles and URLs and supports switching, closing, and restoring tabs; tabGroups reads and restores native group metadata. The extension does not read page contents or modify bookmarks.

Questions: https://github.com/daniele-f/saved-tabs-extension/issues
