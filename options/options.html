Saved tab groups

Active groups are open in this Firefox window. Closing a group saves a local restorable copy under Inactive groups.

Available panels

  • Toolbar popup: compact, one-click group view. Firefox controls where its button appears.
  • Sidebar: searchable, persistent group browser.

Open the sidebar

To open the popup, click the extension’s toolbar button. Use Firefox Customize Toolbar to move it out of the overflow menu or hide it.
