Saved Tab Groups — UX Playground

Twenty temporary test groups are loaded to check scrolling. Real browser tabs are never read or changed.

Compare approaches

  • Toolbar popup: compact, one-click group controls. Firefox controls where its button appears.
  • Sidebar: searchable, persistent group browser.

Always available

The toolbar popup is initially placed on Firefox’s bookmarks toolbar, but Firefox and the user control its actual placement. The sidebar can be opened and closed by the user.

To open the popup, click the extension’s toolbar button. Use Firefox Customize Toolbar to move it out of the overflow menu or hide it.
